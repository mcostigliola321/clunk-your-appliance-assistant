## Product and interface truth

Clunk is a consumer-first visual appliance guide. A homeowner and browser agent share one deterministic repair state. The agent organizes source-backed lookup and navigation; the person supplies physical observations. Model identity, symptom guidance, exact-part compatibility, seller availability, and promoted placement remain separate claims.

The user-pinned direction is **Approachable Precision**: calm paper, graphite, tide green, restrained citron, recognizable appliance cutaways, and ruled divisions. Real diagnosis is primary. Four appliance choices lead to problem, model, location, observation, and answer. Finished fixtures remain behind **See a finished guide**. Technical evidence stays secondary.

Customer language is **Exact part available**, **Safe checks available**, and **Exact part currently unavailable**. Exact matches collapse to one decisive choice; seller offers use **View offer**. Do not expose evidence-tier jargon in the main journey. See `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md`.

The homepage demo sits inside **One guide. Two ways to use it.**, below the person/browser explanation and above the inspector. **Watch the 2:30 demo** creates a YouTube privacy-enhanced iframe only on click. No autoplay or initial YouTube request. Closing the video or parent disclosure unmounts it. A direct YouTube fallback remains available. The primary appliance layout, repair state, and tools are unchanged. See `docs/architecture.md`.
